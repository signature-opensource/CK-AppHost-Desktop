Engine of [CK.AppHost.DesktopNgApp](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-AppHost-Desktop/blob/stable/CK.AppHost.DesktopNgApp/README.md): makes the endpoint of a desktop application in process.

`DesktopNgAppAppBuilderContributor` projects `"HttpEndpoint": { "InProcess": true }` in the party of the App whose client
enables the "DesktopNgApp" option of its "NgApp" feature, and refuses two such clients in an App.

See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-AppHost-Desktop/blob/stable/CK.AppHost.DesktopNgApp.Engine/README.md).
